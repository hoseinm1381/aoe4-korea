# Korea 0.3.4

The duplicate cavalry props came from a legacy visual fallback that replaced left-hand shield attachments with weapons. The native combat hardpoints still provided the unit's actual weapons. Six Korean attachment lists now use the original shields in every affected variant. Eight Korean unit actors reference private weapon visual lists through their existing native combat slots. Horsemen use a fighting spear and a separate charge spear. Knights use a fighting sabre and a separate charge lance, with matching alternatives for the polearm upgrade. Mounted Hwarang use a Korean-styled bow with the native draw rig. Weapon gameplay references, event owners, animation selectors, and stats are preserved. The Korean weapon designs were adapted to the stock weapon geometry and atlases to retain exact grip, skin, markers, and charge effects.

Tiger Hunters have fitted plate rows, more prominent waist and neck trim, and progressive helmets across Feudal, Castle, and Imperial. The iron helmet and neck guard are inspired by [Joseon armor in the National Museum of Korea](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0403000000.do?relicId=4461&schM=view&searchId=represent). The three original animated owners supply the 48-bone bind records and native rendering selectors. Each tier has three active model LODs. The preview in `docs/update_034/tiger-readback.png` shows the cooked body in rest pose and illustrates the team colour mask.

## Source and verification

The three Blender masters and shader atlases, plus weapon paint masters and recipes, are in `authoring/art034`. `tools/art034` contains the Tiger Hunter authoring/export, weapon derivation, compose, and readback tools. To recook the Tiger Hunter masters, use the existing `tools/art033/stage_cook.py` staging recipe with the three Tiger Hunter IDs, then use the command-line Garrison Content Editor model cook. The compose tool keeps the existing private actor materials, LOD partitions, and exact bind matrices while incorporating the recooked models and textures. `BUILD.cmd` assembles the complete playable ZIP from indexed editable and native inputs without recooking the art.

The Content Editor cook, candidate model readback, native data validation, complete member SHA-256 and archive identity checks, and ZIP verification passed. A fresh match is still needed to confirm the charge-to-melee swap, Hwarang bow draw, and Tiger Hunter appearance and animation at gameplay camera distance.
