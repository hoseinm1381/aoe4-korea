# Korean 0.2.0 linguistic evidence, 2026-11-02

The owner requested reconstructed Korean that changes across ages. No historical
pronunciation qualification has yet been established for the selected models.
Qwen lists Korean among its modern supported languages and offers voice design
and cloning. It does not document an Old Korean or Middle Korean language pack,
a historical phoneme inventory, or validated reconstruction controls.

The civ card spans Three Kingdoms to Joseon. The production-plan age labels are
an editorial alignment with that range, not four independently attested dialects.
Old Korean is commonly associated with Silla through the tenth century. Goryeo
represents Early Middle Korean; the first two Joseon centuries represent Late
Middle Korean. Early speech must be reconstructed from fragmentary records with
later evidence. A complete game command sentence cannot be called attested just
because a constituent word is old. The transition bank requires an explicit
editorial rationale rather than a fictitious new language period.

The 1446 Hunminjeongeum explanation documents letter sounds and usage and provides
a stronger basis for the final age. Its evidence does not validate a modern TTS
engine's rendering of archaic letters. Modern-Hangul respelling, archaic endings,
actor delivery and digital pitch changes cannot establish recovered phonology.
Every production record distinguishes intended historical form, actual synthesis
input, meaning, source locator, confidence and remaining sound errors in
authoring/voices020/VOICE_SCRIPTS.json. These are original composed game lines;
the sources establish constituent forms and period limits, not full utterance attestation.

The scheduled job expressly permits historical and native-speaker review to remain
pending. Production is proceeding as a candidly labelled approximation under that
scope. The owner explicitly confirmed "Use researched approximations" in the current
chat. This authorizes the documented candidate approach and is not a claim of
verified historical pronunciation. Age 1 is explicitly starred as an
uncertain retrojection; age 2 uses a Goryeo projection; age 3 is an editorial
late-Goryeo/early-Joseon bridge; age 4 is Middle-Korean-inspired. All four retain
modern-model pronunciation limits. No bank is called authentic reconstructed speech.

No real person is impersonated. Thirty-two fictional voice designs provide
consistent role and gender identity. Age variation is in the researched script
and independently synthesized takes, rather than a claim that pitch changes
recover lost phonology. The modern reference transcript is only a technical
speaker anchor and is not used as an age-specific game line.
